Job-site login

One email and password for every job site (Workday, amazon.jobs and others). Job Hunter signs in with it, or creates the account when the site has none. Use a password you use nowhere else; "Make one" creates a strong one. It is encrypted and kept only in this browser, never sent to your Job Hunter app, and only typed into the job site's own Sign In / Create Account forms, never a Google, Microsoft or LinkedIn sign-in. When a site emails a confirmation link or code, Job Hunter reads that one email in Gmail (signed in in this Chrome as this address) and uses it.
